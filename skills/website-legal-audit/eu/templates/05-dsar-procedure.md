# DSAR handling procedure — {{CONTROLLER_NAME}}

## Intake
- Channel: {{CONTROLLER_EMAIL}} [if DPO_CONTACT] / DPO: {{DPO_CONTACT}} [/if].
- Log every request: date received, subject, type of right, identity check.

## Rights covered (Art. 15–22)
Access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, objection, and rights
related to automated decision-making.

## Steps
1. **Verify identity** of the requester (proportionate, no excessive data).
2. **Classify** the request (which right).
3. **Locate data** across systems and processors.
4. **Respond within one month** of receipt (Art. 12(3)); extendable by two
   further months for complex/numerous requests — inform the subject within the
   first month.
5. **Free of charge** by default; a fee/refusal only for manifestly unfounded or
   excessive requests, with justification.
6. **Document** the outcome and actions taken.

## Deadlines summary
| Event | Deadline |
|-------|----------|
| Acknowledge / respond | 1 month |
| Extension (complex) | +2 months, notify within month 1 |
| Breach notification to authority | 72 hours (separate, Art. 33) |
